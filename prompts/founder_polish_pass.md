# Founder Polish Pass

Audit OpenMediaForge as if it is launching to serious creators this week.

Check:

- Does the product feel like a premium creative workstation?
- Is the first-run mock demo clear and impressive?
- Are provider/key/status states visible and trustworthy?
- Are placeholders honest?
- Are jobs and receipts easy to understand?
- Does the Image Studio feel useful, not decorative?
- Are asset cards attractive and functional?
- Is there any generic SaaS sludge?
- Is there any fake compute implication?
- Is BYOK clearly KeyRail, not a casual key field?

Improve copy, spacing, hierarchy, empty states, card design, and trust language.

Run verification after changes.
